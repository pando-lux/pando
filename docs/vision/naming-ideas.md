## THEME 1: "OPEN" (Transparency, Openness, Nothing Hidden)

| Name | Meaning | "Search it on ___" | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Aperture** | Literal meaning: an opening. Also a camera lens opening that lets light in. | "Search it on Aperture" | Visual, scientific, transparency + clarity in one word | A bit long. Portal 2 fans will think of the game. |
| **Candor** | Openness, honesty, frankness | "Ask Candor" | Directly means what the network IS. Unique. | Might sound like a company name. Hunger Games district. |
| **Lucid** | Clear, transparent, easy to understand | "Search Lucid" | Clean, modern, implies clarity of thought | Lucid Motors exists (EV company) |
| **Libre** | Free/open (as in freedom, not price) | "Built on Libre" | Strong freedom connotation. Used in open-source world (LibreOffice). | Very "open source nerd" — might not feel mainstream |
| **Pando** | Latin: "I spread, I open, I extend" | "Search Pando" | **Also the world's largest living organism** — 47,000 aspen trees connected by ONE root system underground. One organism, thousands of visible trees, all connected invisibly. EXACTLY what this network is. Short, unique, memorable. | Less known word. Needs explanation initially. |
| **Agora** | Ancient Greek: public marketplace + assembly | "Find it on Agora" | Where democracy literally happened. Where people traded, debated, connected. The original "open network." Historical gravitas. | Might sound academic. Some crypto projects use it. |

**My pick from this theme: Pando.** The metaphor is perfect — one living network, thousands of nodes, all connected underground. And the Latin meaning is literally "I open, I spread."

## CURRENCY NAME OPTIONS

The currency should feel like something you EARN through useful work. Short, 3-4 letters.

### If we like "Lux" direction (light-based):

| Name | Meaning | "That costs 50 ___" | Feel |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Lux** | Light (Latin) | "That costs 50 Lux" | Elegant, valuable, clean |
| **Sol** | Sun | "I earned 200 Sol" | Warm, universal, powerful. (Solana uses SOL though) |
| **Ray** | Beam of light | "50 Rays" | Simple, visual, accessible |
| **Glow** | Gentle light | "Earned 100 Glow" | Warm, positive, inviting |
| **Arc** | Path of light | "That costs 10 Arc" | Clean, mathematical, short |

### Work/value-based:

| Name | Meaning | "That costs 50 ___" | Feel |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Merit** | Earned through useful work | "I earned 500 Merit" | Exactly what it is. You merit it. |
| **Ore** | Raw valuable material | "50 Ore" | Short, valuable, fundamental. Also Scandinavian small currency unit. |
| **Proof** | Evidence of work done | "That costs 30 Proof" | Ties to "proof of useful work." Earned, not given. |
| **Cred** | From "credential" / credibility | "Earned 200 Cred" | Trust-based. You have cred = you're credible. |

## MY TOP COMBINATIONS

### 1. **Pando** + **Lux**
- "Search it on Pando." "I earned 500 Lux."
- The world's largest living organism (connected root system) + light
- Open, spreading, interconnected + valuable, bright, clear
- Both words are short, global, memorable

### 2. **Pando** + **Merit**
- "Built on Pando." "That costs 50 Merit."
- The connected organism + earned through useful work
- Organic metaphor + what the currency literally represents

### 3. **Verity** + **Lux**
- "Verity it." "Pay 20 Lux."
- Truth + light
- Becomes a verb naturally ("I'll verity that" = I'll check if it's true)

### 4. **Agora** + **Merit**
- "Find it on Agora." "Earned 300 Merit."
- The original open marketplace of democracy + earned value

### 5. **Vero** + **Lux**
- "Search Vero." "Costs 10 Lux."
- True (in Italian/Spanish) + light
- International, short, clean
